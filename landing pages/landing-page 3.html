FAQs

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Groove Folios is free and open source under the GPL. No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no catch.

Can I create templates for my brand?

Yes. Groove includes a theming system so you can standardize the look and feel of your content across your organization.

What if I need PDF files?

Groove can generate PDF exports automatically, so you can still share static files when you need to.

Does it work with my existing WordPress site?

Yes. Install it like any other plugin. It uses the Block Editor and stores content in your own database — no external services required.

Can I self-host everything?

Absolutely. Your content lives entirely on your server. No third-party accounts, no embeds, no vendor lock-in.

How do I get started?

Install the plugin, create a folio, pick a theme, and publish. You'll be live in under a minute.
